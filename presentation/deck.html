What humans still own

Choosing what matters
Defining "done"
Designing the factory & its guardrails
Owning the outcome
Your first hires should build the line, not stand on it.

One line each. Today you chose what mattered by voting. I designed the factory. If it breaks production, that's on me, not the AI.

Then the banner: same goes for your first hires. Hire one person to answer every support email and you've bought one worker. Hire them to set up how support gets answered, and keep improving it, and you've bought a factory. Hire builders, not line workers.
